The Twelve Specifications, Gathered

    Read together, the twelve describe a single teaching: the body is temporary, every experience instructs, nothing is wasted, unlearned patterns return, growth has no final stop, presence outranks place, others mirror the self, the mind authors both bondage and freedom, and the answer — forgotten, then remembered — was always within.

    — Prakash Hegade

# The Learning Explainer: Connecting the Nodes

Welcome to your structured learning repository. This guide connects the dots between the various topics you've explored.

## 1. Foundations: Communication & Control
Everything starts with how machines talk. 
- **SSH & Protocols:** Learn how to establish secure tunnels and the history of communication from Telnet to modern SSH.
- **Git:** Understand the design of the world's most popular version control system. It's not just "saving code"—it's a cryptographic data structure.

## 2. Infrastructure: Containers & Networking
Modern development lives in containers.
- **Isolation:** How Linux namespaces and cgroups keep processes in their own "bubbles."
- **Networking:** Deep dive into ports, forwarding, and firewalls. This is where security meets functionality.

## 3. Intelligence: Math & Algorithms
Underneath every piece of software is math.
- **Probability:** Explore how reinforcement models like Polya's Urn explain behavior in both nature and software.
- **DSA:** Moving from simple loops to Dynamic Programming and advanced storage options.

## 4. Web & AI: The Applications
This is where the theory meets the user.
- **MERN Stack:** Building full-stack applications with JavaScript.
- **ML & Data:** Using data exploration to make sense of the world.

# Probability & Math Concepts

## 1. Bag Theory (Polya's Urn Model)
Polya's Urn is a powerful model in probability that demonstrates **reinforcement**.
- **The Experiment:** You have an urn with red and blue balls. You draw one. You return it AND add another ball of the same color.
- **Insight:** This creates a "rich-get-richer" effect. The more red balls you draw, the higher the probability of drawing red in the future. It's used to model things like market share or population growth.

## 2. Reinforcement Strategy
In the context of probability and AI, a reinforcement strategy is about rewarding certain outcomes to increase their likelihood in the future. In the Urn model, "adding a ball of the same color" is the reinforcement.

## 3. Polya Urn in Software Theory
This can be used to model cache hits (frequently accessed items become more likely to be accessed again) or network traffic patterns.
